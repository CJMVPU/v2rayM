# v0.5.3 solid dashboard motion and depth

- Replace flat flips with two-sided 3D surfaces, layered side thickness,
  perspective, directional shading and depth-sensitive shadows. The original
  face owns layout so the disc stays inside its four traffic rings.
- Activate the disc and all three dashboard cards with a slower 1.54-second
  full turn and slight overshoot/settle. Stopping adds a small 0.54-second
  settling tilt. Respect Reduce Motion.
- Connection rows turn vertically over 1.12 seconds, with independent,
  non-sequential slot phases and identity jitter. Each slot rests at least
  1.8 seconds between turns; updates during a turn coalesce into the latest
  pending record instead of restarting it. Metadata-only changes do not flip.
- Use a stable dark material and relief shadows for all cards, the disc and
  its four corner badges. Side lighting fades away when the surface rests.
- Remove the duplicated connection-duration row from the status card. Keep
  the live duration in the disc; give the recovered space to seven centered
  recent-connection rows with symmetric insets.
